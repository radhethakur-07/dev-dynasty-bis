# 23 EV Charging and Battery Standards

Source: https://standards.bis.gov.in/

## Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure & Safety

Bureau of Indian Standards has formulated a comprehensive suite of standards for Electric Vehicles (EVs) under the Electrotechnical Division Council. Major notified standards include:
- **IS 17017 (Part 1):2018**: Electric Vehicle Conductive Charging System — General Requirements. Covers safety interlocks, protection against electric shock, insulation resistance, and standardized communication protocols between EV and EVSE.
- **IS 17017 (Part 2/Sec 1):2020**: Standardized plugs, sockets, and vehicle connectors for AC and DC charging.
- **IS 17017 (Part 22):2021**: AC Smart Charging Stations.
- **IS 17017 (Part 23):2021**: DC Fast Charging Stations up to 250 kW.
- **IS 17855:2022**: Mandatory safety and performance testing for Lithium-ion traction battery packs and systems (thermal runaway test, overcharge cutoff, vibration, and mechanical drop tests).

Certification Pathway: Manufacturers must obtain Scheme I (ISI Mark) or Scheme II (CRS) certification by submitting test reports from BIS-recognized test laboratories (such as ARAI, CPRI, or ERDA).

## Battery Swapping & Interoperability Standards

To support urban electric two-wheelers and three-wheelers, BIS notified interoperable battery swapping standards:
- Standards ensure dimensional interchangeability, standardized locking mechanisms, and universal Battery Management System (BMS) communication.
- Rigorous ingress protection (minimum IP67) and thermal propagation containment required for all swappable packs.
