# Household Products — Indian Standards and BIS Certification

## Overview
BIS certifies numerous household products under Scheme I (ISI Mark) to ensure safety and quality for consumers. Quality Control Orders cover domestic pressure cookers, gas stoves, LPG equipment, water storage tanks, and other essential household items.

## Kitchen and Cooking
- **IS 2347:2017**: Domestic Pressure Cookers. Compulsory under Domestic Pressure Cooker (Quality Control) Order. Mandatory testing includes operating pressure (1.0 kg/cm²), burst pressure (≥4× working pressure), safety relief valve, handle strength, and gasket durability.
- **IS 4246:2002**: Domestic Gas Stoves for use with LPG. Compulsory under Gas Stoves QCO. Tests include gas consumption, thermal efficiency (≥68%), flame stability, and anti-tilting.
- **IS 9798**: Low Pressure Regulators for LPG mixtures. Compulsory under Scheme I.
- **IS 8468:2015**: Domestic LPG Cylinders. Compulsory under Scheme I.

## Water Storage and Purification
- **IS 12252:2006**: Polyethylene Water Storage Tanks. Compulsory under Scheme I. Covers overhead and ground-level tanks from 100 to 10,000 litres. Mandatory food-grade polyethylene requirements.
- **IS 14543:2024**: Packaged Drinking Water. Compulsory under Scheme I.
- **IS 16240:2014**: Gravity-based Domestic Water Purifiers. Specifies performance requirements for non-electric purifiers.

## Bathroom and Plumbing
- **IS 781:2019**: Waste Cocks — Specification. Covers brass, bronze, and gunmetal waste cocks for bathrooms.
- **IS 2692:2015**: Ferrule Cocks. Specification for brass ferrule cocks used in plumbing.
- **IS 15778:2018**: Chlorinated PVC (CPVC) Pipes for Hot and Cold Water Distribution. Compulsory.
- **IS 15328:2003**: PVC Pipes for Water Supply.
- **IS 2556 (Parts 1 to 3)**: Vitreous Sanitary Appliances (Wash basins, WC pans, urinals).

## Furniture and Fittings
- **IS 4905:2017**: Padlocks — Specification. Covers pin tumbler padlocks in various security grades.
- **IS 2062**: Steel used in furniture and fittings manufacturing.
- **IS 13414**: Stainless Steel Utensils for Domestic Use.

## Home Safety
- **IS 15301:2017**: Domestic Smoke Alarms. Specifies sensitivity, alarm level, and battery life.
- **IS 2171:2018**: Portable Fire Extinguishers. Compulsory for commercial buildings.
- **IS 2190:2010**: Selection, Installation, and Maintenance of First-Aid Fire Extinguishers.

## Labelling and Consumer Rights
Under the BIS Act, 2016:
- All ISI-marked products must display the Standard Mark, licence number, IS number, and manufacturing date
- Consumers can verify ISI licence authenticity on bis.gov.in
- Complaints can be filed through BIS CARE or consumer helpline 1800-11-4000
