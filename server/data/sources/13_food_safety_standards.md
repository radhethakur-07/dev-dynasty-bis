# Food Safety Standards — Indian Standards, FSSAI, and BIS Compulsory Certification

## Overview
Food safety in India is regulated jointly by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) and the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). Several food product standards are mandated under BIS Scheme I (ISI Mark) by Quality Control Orders. FSSAI references Indian Standards in its Food Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulations.

## Packaged Water
- **IS 14543:2024**: Packaged Drinking Water (Other than Packaged Natural Mineral Water). Compulsory under BIS Scheme I. Specifies requirements for microbiological, chemical, and physical quality parameters. All manufacturers must have BIS licence and display ISI Mark. Mandatory factory-level in-house testing laboratory.
- **IS 13428:2024**: Packaged Natural Mineral Water. Compulsory under BIS Scheme I. Covers spring water, artesian well water, and other natural mineral water sources. Requires source-specific mineral analysis.

## Milk and Dairy Products
- **IS 1165:2016**: Milk Powder — Whole and Skim. Referenced by FSSAI. Specifies fat content, moisture, acidity, and microbiological limits.
- **IS 1166:2017**: Condensed Milk (Sweetened and Unsweetened). Referenced by FSSAI regulations.
- **IS 5765 (Part 1):2010**: Infant Milk Food based on cow or buffalo milk. Referenced by FSSAI. Mandatory testing for protein, fat, moisture, and contaminant limits.

## Edible Oils and Fats
- **IS 548 (Part 1):2023**: Methods of sampling and test for oils and fats. Foundational standard used across all edible oil testing.
- **IS 3657**: Mustard Oil (3rd Revision). Referenced by FSSAI.
- **IS 8322**: Blended Edible Vegetable Oils. Referenced by FSSAI for blended oil standards.

## Cereals and Pulses
- **IS 4333 (Part 1)**: Methods of Analysis of Foodgrains — Physical examination.
- **IS 4333 (Part 2)**: Methods of Analysis of Foodgrains — Determination of moisture content.
- **IS 7419**: Wheat Flour (Atta, Maida, Suji, Sooji).

## Food Contact Materials and Packaging
- **IS 10146:2011**: Polyethylene for its Safe Use in Contact with Foodstuffs, Pharmaceuticals and Drinking Water. Compulsory under QCO.
- **IS 10142:1999**: Polypropylene and its copolymers for safe use in contact with foodstuffs. Compulsory.
- **IS 10151:1999**: PVC and its copolymers for safe use in contact with foodstuffs. Compulsory.
- **IS 15495:2020**: Water-based food-grade greases.

## Spices
- **IS 1797:2005**: Ground Black Pepper (Kali Mirch).
- **IS 7318:2005**: Turmeric (Haldi) Powder — Specification.
- **IS 8933**: Chilli Powder — Specification.
- **IS 2105**: Red Chillies, Whole — Specification.

## Food Safety Testing Requirements
BIS-certified food products must undergo:
1. Physical testing (moisture, foreign matter)
2. Chemical testing (pH, acidity, pesticide residues, heavy metals)
3. Microbiological testing (total plate count, E. coli, coliform, yeast, mould)
4. Sensory evaluation (taste, odour, appearance)
5. Packaging compliance (migration limits for food-contact materials)

Testing must be done at BIS-recognized / NABL-accredited laboratories.
