# BIS Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS) FAQ

Source status: Official BIS Public Information.
Official source URL: https://www.bis.gov.in/fmcs/fmcs-faqs/?lang=en

## Scope of FMCS & Governing Regulations

- Scheme Basis: FMCS operates under Scheme-I of Schedule-II of the Bureau of Indian Standards (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018.
- Purpose: Grants licence to foreign manufacturers located outside India to use the Standard Mark (ISI Mark) on products exported to the Indian market.
- Scope: Covers both voluntary standards and products notified under Compulsory Quality Control Orders (QCOs) by Government of India ministries.

## Key Requirements for Foreign Applicants

1. Authorized Indian Representative (AIR):
   - Every foreign applicant must nominate an Authorized Indian Representative (AIR) who is an Indian resident or an entity incorporated in India.
   - The AIR is legally responsible for compliance with the BIS Act, 2016, rules, regulations, and terms of the license agreement.
2. In-house Manufacturing & Testing Facility:
   - The foreign manufacturing unit must have independent manufacturing facilities and complete testing equipment as per the relevant Indian Standard.
3. Factory Audit by BIS Officers:
   - BIS technical officers visit the manufacturing premises abroad to audit manufacturing infrastructure, test facilities, quality control systems, and draw verification samples.
4. Independent Testing in India:
   - Verification samples drawn during the foreign factory inspection are tested in BIS recognized laboratories in India.
5. Financial Compliance:
   - Upon successful verification, the foreign manufacturer must submit a Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG) and pay applicable marking fees prior to license grant.
