# BIS Product Certification Process & FAQ

Source status: Official BIS Public Information.
Official source URL: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/product-certification-faq/?lang=en
Application Portal: https://www.manakonline.in

## Product Certification Process & Grant of Licence

- Scheme Overview: BIS operates Product Certification Scheme (Scheme-I) under BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018 for grant of licence to use the Standard Mark (ISI Mark).
- Online Application: Manufacturers must register and apply through the official Manakonline e-portal (manakonline.in) along with application fee and required documents.
- Infrastructure Prerequisites: The manufacturing unit must have necessary manufacturing machinery, complete in-house testing facilities as per Scheme of Inspection and Testing (SIT), and qualified technical quality control personnel.

## Certification Pathways: Normal vs Simplified Procedure

1. Normal Procedure (Option 1):
   - Preliminary factory audit conducted by BIS officers.
   - Verification of manufacturing infrastructure, quality control system, and in-house testing laboratory.
   - Drawing of official verification samples during audit for testing in BIS recognized laboratory.
   - Grant of CM/L (Certification Mark Licence) upon receiving passing test reports and compliance with SIT.

2. Simplified Procedure (Option 2):
   - Available for select products where applicant gets sample pre-tested from a BIS-recognized testing laboratory.
   - Application submitted with pre-testing report, factory layout, and manufacturing details.
   - Licence granted after verification of application documents and factory audit, reducing grant timeline.

## Continuous Surveillance & Responsibilities

- Once granted, licence is valid for 1 to 2 years and renewable upon payment of marking fees.
- Licensees must follow the prescribed Scheme of Inspection and Testing (SIT) and maintain routine test records.
- BIS conducts unannounced periodic factory surveillance audits and draws market surveillance samples from retail channels to ensure continuous compliance.
- Misuse of ISI mark or selling non-conforming goods is a cognizable offense under Section 29 of the BIS Act, 2016.
