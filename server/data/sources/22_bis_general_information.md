# BIS General Information — Organization, Services, and Consumer Resources

## About the Bureau of Indian Standards
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is the National Standards Body of India, established under the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016. BIS operates under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution. BIS headquarters are located in Manak Bhavan, 9 Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg, New Delhi — 110002.

## Key Functions of BIS
1. **Standards Formulation**: BIS formulates Indian Standards through Technical Committees comprising representatives from industry, government, consumer organizations, and technical institutions. As of 2024, BIS has published over 22,000 Indian Standards across 14 sectors.
2. **Product Certification (Conformity Assessment)**: BIS operates multiple certification schemes:
   - **Scheme I (ISI Mark)**: For products under compulsory or voluntary certification. Requires factory inspection, testing at BIS-recognized labs, and continuous surveillance.
   - **Scheme II (CRS)**: Compulsory Registration Scheme for electronics and IT goods. No factory inspection required.
   - **Scheme IV (CoC)**: Certificate of Conformity for imported goods. Ensures imported products meet Indian Standards.
   - **Scheme V (SMIS)**: Standard Mark for Indian Standards for import goods.
   - **Scheme X (FMCS)**: Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme for overseas manufacturers.
3. **Hallmarking**: BIS operates the hallmarking scheme for gold and silver jewellery to certify purity.
4. **Laboratory Services**: BIS recognizes testing laboratories under the Laboratory Recognition Scheme (LRS) and operates the LIMS portal.
5. **Standards Promotion**: Through Standards Clubs, BIS promotes awareness of standardization among students and industry.
6. **Training**: BIS provides training programs on standardization, quality management, and conformity assessment.

## How to Apply for BIS Certification
### Scheme I (ISI Mark) Application Process
1. **Step 1**: Visit manakonline.in and register
2. **Step 2**: Select the relevant Indian Standard and product category
3. **Step 3**: Upload required documents:
   - Factory layout plan
   - Manufacturing process details
   - List of testing equipment
   - Quality control procedures
   - Previous test reports (if any)
4. **Step 4**: Pay the application fee online
5. **Step 5**: BIS officer conducts factory inspection
6. **Step 6**: Product samples collected for testing at BIS-recognized lab
7. **Step 7**: If tests pass and factory complies, licence is granted
8. **Step 8**: Licence is valid until cancelled or suspended; annual marking fee and surveillance continue

### Fees (Indicative, subject to revision)
- Application fee: ₹1,000
- Annual marking fee: Based on annual production value (minimum ₹1,000 to maximum ₹5,00,000)
- Testing fee: Varies by product and laboratory

## Simplified Procedure for Grant of Licence
BIS has introduced a Simplified Procedure for micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) to obtain ISI certification faster. Under this procedure:
- Reduced documentation requirements
- Expedited factory inspection
- Lower testing fees for first-time applicants
- Online tracking of application status
- Faster processing (target: 60 days for MSME applications)

## Consumer Complaints
Consumers who suspect a product bearing the ISI Mark or BIS Registration Mark is of substandard quality can:
1. File a complaint online at https://www.bis.gov.in/consumer-affairs/
2. Call BIS CARE helpline: 1800-11-4000 (toll-free)
3. Email: biscare@bis.gov.in
4. Visit the nearest BIS Branch Office
BIS investigates complaints, can draw product samples for testing, and take enforcement action including licence cancellation.

## BIS Regional and Branch Offices
BIS has 5 Regional Offices and 38 Branch Offices across India:
- **Northern Region**: Delhi (HQ), Chandigarh, Jaipur, Lucknow, Dehradun, Jammu, Patna
- **Southern Region**: Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Thiruvananthapuram, Kochi, Visakhapatnam
- **Eastern Region**: Kolkata, Bhubaneswar, Guwahati, Ranchi
- **Western Region**: Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Pune, Bhopal, Nagpur, Rajkot
- **Central Office**: New Delhi — Manak Bhavan
