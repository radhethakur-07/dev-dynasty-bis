# Electrical Appliances — Indian Standards and QCO Compulsory Certification

## Overview
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) mandates compulsory certification for numerous electrical appliances under Scheme I (ISI Mark). The Electrical Equipment (Quality Control) Orders issued by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs and the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) require that specified electrical products conform to designated Indian Standards before manufacture, sale, import, or distribution in India.

## Domestic Electrical Appliances

### Wiring and Cables
- **IS 694:2010**: PVC Insulated Cables for working voltages up to and including 1100 V. Compulsory under Scheme I (ISI Mark). Covers single-core, multi-core, flexible, and flat cables for domestic and industrial wiring installations.
- **IS 10810 (Part 1):1984/ISO 6722**: Insulated cables for automobiles — Requirements and test methods.
- **IS 1554 (Part 1):2022**: PVC Insulated (Heavy Duty) Electric Cables for voltages up to and including 1100V. Compulsory under Scheme I.
- **IS 7098 (Part 1):2020**: Cross-linked Polyethylene Insulated PVC Sheathed Cables for voltages up to and including 1100V. Compulsory under Scheme I.

### Switches and Sockets
- **IS 3854:1997**: Switches for domestic and similar purposes. Compulsory under Scheme I. Covers single-pole, two-pole, and three-pole switches rated up to 16 A, 250 V.
- **IS 1293:2019**: Plugs, Socket-Outlets and Socket-Outlet Adaptors for Domestic and Similar Purposes. Compulsory under Scheme I. Covers 6 A and 16 A rated plugs and sockets.

### Fans
- **IS 374:2019**: Ceiling Fans — Specification. Compulsory under Scheme I. Covers sweep sizes 600 mm to 1400 mm. Mandatory testing includes air delivery, service value, power consumption, and safety.

### Electric Irons
- **IS 302 (Part 2, Section 3):2008**: Electric Irons — Safety requirements. Compulsory under Scheme I. Applies to electric irons for household or similar purposes.

### Water Heaters and Geysers
- **IS 302 (Part 2, Section 21):2009**: Storage Water Heaters. Compulsory under Scheme I. Covers immersion-type and storage-type water heaters for domestic use. Mandatory tests include electrical safety, thermal cut-off, and earth continuity.

### LED and Lighting
- **IS 10322 (Part 5/Section 1):2013**: Luminaires — General Requirements and Tests. Compulsory under Scheme I.
- **IS 16102 (Part 1):2012**: Self-Ballasted LED Lamps for General Lighting Services — Safety. Compulsory under Scheme I.
- **IS 16102 (Part 2):2012**: Self-Ballasted LED Lamps — Performance Requirements. Compulsory under Scheme I.

### MCBs and Switchgear
- **IS 8828:2018**: Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCBs) for rated voltages not exceeding 253V. Compulsory under Scheme I.
- **IS/IEC 60947-2:2016**: Low-Voltage Switchgear and Controlgear — Circuit Breakers.

## Safety Testing Requirements
All electrical appliances under compulsory certification must undergo:
1. Type testing at a BIS-recognized laboratory
2. Factory inspection by BIS officers
3. Periodic surveillance and sample testing
4. Marking compliance (Standard Mark / ISI Mark on product and packaging)
