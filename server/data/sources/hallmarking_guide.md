# BIS Hallmarking & HUID — Initial Ingestion Notes

Source status: Official BIS public information.

## Official Hallmarking FAQ

https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/hallmarking-faqs/hallmarking-faq/

Current official FAQ facts to index:
- HUID means Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) number.
- HUID is a six-digit alphanumeric number and is unique for each hallmarked item.
- Consumers can verify HUID using the BIS Care App's "Verify HUID" feature.
- BIS states that, since the introduction of HUID on 1 July 2021, hallmark on gold jewellery consists of three marks: BIS logo, purity of the article in caratage and fineness, and six-digit alphanumeric HUID number.

## Consumer Protection / Verification

https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/consumer-protection/?lang=en

BIS states that:
- Consumers can have jewellery/sample tested at BIS Recognized Assaying & Hallmarking Centres on a chargeable basis.
- BIS provides guidance concerning testing and compensation in cases where hallmarked jewellery is found to have lower purity than declared.
- HUID authenticity can be checked through the BIS Care App.
