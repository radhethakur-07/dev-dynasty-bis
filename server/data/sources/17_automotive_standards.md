# Automotive Standards — Indian Standards for Vehicles and Components

## Overview
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) publishes standards for automotive components and accessories. Several automotive products are under compulsory certification through Quality Control Orders issued by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH). BIS works in conjunction with the Automotive Research Association of India (ARAI) and other type-approval agencies.

## Helmets
- **IS 4151:2015**: Protective Helmets for Two-Wheeler Riders. Compulsory under Helmet (Quality Control) Order, 2018. Mandatory testing includes impact absorption, penetration resistance, chinstrap retention, field of vision, and labelling.

## Safety Glass
- **IS 2553 (Part 1):2019**: Safety Glass for Road Transport Vehicles — Specification (Front Windshield). Compulsory under Safety Glass QCO.
- **IS 2553 (Part 2):2019**: Safety Glass for Road Transport Vehicles — Specification (Side, Rear, and Roof Windows). Compulsory.

## Tyres
- **IS 15633:2018**: Automotive Pneumatic Tyres for Passenger Cars. Compulsory under Pneumatic Tyres and Tubes QCO.
- **IS 15636:2018**: Automotive Pneumatic Tyres for Commercial Vehicles. Compulsory.
- **IS 13098:2013**: Automotive Pneumatic Tyres for Two and Three Wheeled Motor Vehicles. Compulsory.
- **IS 15627:2005**: Pneumatic Tyres for Agricultural Tractors.

## Automobile Accessories
- **IS 2062**: Hot Rolled Steel (used in automotive body/chassis manufacturing). Compulsory under Steel QCO.
- **IS 1732**: Rear View Mirrors for Motor Vehicles.
- **IS 10262**: Concrete Mix Proportioning (relevant for road infrastructure).

## Vehicle Batteries
- **IS 14257:2020**: Automotive Vehicles — Storage Batteries (Lead-Acid type for starting, lighting, ignition). Referenced in Central Motor Vehicle Rules.
- **IS 16046 (Part 2)**: Lithium-Ion Batteries for Electric Vehicles. Under CRS.

## Automotive Lighting
- **IS 10119 (Part 1)**: Motor Vehicle Headlamps — Specification.
- **IS 6381**: Motor Vehicle Direction Indicators.
- **IS 2552**: Reflex Reflectors for Vehicles.

## Fasteners and Components
- **IS 1363 (Parts 1 to 3)**: Hexagon Head Bolts, Screws, and Nuts (ISO metric). Widely used in automotive assembly.
- **IS 1367 (Parts 1 to 20)**: Technical Supply Conditions for Threaded Steel Fasteners.

## Testing Requirements
Automotive components under BIS certification must be tested at recognized laboratories for:
1. Impact and crash-worthiness (helmets, safety glass)
2. Road performance (tyres — speed rating, load index, endurance)
3. Environmental resistance (corrosion, temperature cycling)
4. Electrical safety (batteries, lighting)
5. Material composition (steel grade, rubber compound)

Key BIS-recognized testing laboratories for automotive products include ARAI (Pune), CIRT (Pune), and ICAT (Manesar).
