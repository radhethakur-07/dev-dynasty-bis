# BIS Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS) — Scheme II

Source status: Official BIS Public Information.
Official source URL: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/products-under-compulsory-certification/scheme-ii-registration-scheme/?lang=en
CRS Portal: https://www.crsbis.in/BIS/

## Scheme-II Registration Overview

- Conformity Model: Self-declaration of conformity scheme governed under Scheme-II of the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018.
- Applicability: Primarily covers Electronic, Information Technology (IT), and Solar Photovoltaic goods notified under Compulsory Registration Orders (CRO) by MeitY and MNRE.
- Common Products: Laptops, Tablets, Mobile Phones, Power Banks, Smart Watches, LED Luminaires, Power Adapters, Point of Sale (POS) terminals, and Lithium-ion Secondary Cells.

## Key Process Distinction: No Initial Factory Audit

- Unlike Scheme-I (ISI Mark), Scheme-II does NOT require an on-site factory audit prior to registration.
- Testing Requirement: The manufacturer gets product models tested in a BIS-recognized testing laboratory located in India.
- Test Report Validity: Test reports submitted for registration must not be older than 90 days from the date of issue.
- Grant of Registration: Upon online submission of valid test report, undertaking of compliance, and fee, BIS issues a unique Registration Number.

## Standard Mark for Scheme-II (CRS)

- Products registered under Scheme-II carry the standard words:
  'Self-Declaration — Conforming to IS [Number]' along with the BIS Registration Number 'R-XXXXXXXX' and the official BIS portal reference (www.bis.gov.in).
- Selling, distributing, or importing non-registered notified electronic goods into India is prohibited by law under the MeitY QCO orders.
