# 24 Solar and Renewable Energy Standards

Source: https://www.services.bis.gov.in/

## Mandatory Solar Photovoltaic (PV) QCO Regulations

Under the Solar Photovoltaics, Systems, Devices and Components Goods (Requirements for Compulsory Registration) Order issued by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE):
No solar panels or inverters can be manufactured, imported, or sold in India without mandatory BIS certification.
- **IS 14286 / IEC 61215:2016**: Crystalline Silicon Terrestrial PV Modules — Design Qualification and Type Approval.
- **IS/IEC 61730 (Part 1 & 2):2016**: Photovoltaic Module Safety Qualification (Construction and Testing).
- **IS 16221 (Part 2):2015**: Safety of Utility-Connected Solar Inverters.
- **IS 16169:2014**: Anti-Islanding Protection Test for Solar Inverters.

Licensing Procedure: Manufacturers must submit samples to BIS-approved test labs (such as NISE, CPRI, or ERDA). Upon conforming test reports, BIS grants the Registration Number (R-XXXXXXXX) or ISI Mark licence.
