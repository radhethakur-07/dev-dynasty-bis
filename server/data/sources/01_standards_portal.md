# BIS Standards Master Sources

Purpose: expand the RAG knowledge base from official BIS standards sources.

PRIMARY:
- BIS Standards / Know Your Standard: https://standards.bis.gov.in/
- BIS Know Your Standard: https://www.bis.gov.in/know-your-standard/?lang=en

What to ingest:
- Published Indian Standards
- Standard number / IS number
- Official title
- Product/category
- Status (current/revised/withdrawn, only when explicitly stated)
- Amendments and gazette notifications, when publicly listed
- Scheme of Testing and Inspection (STI), where publicly available
- Related laboratory information, where explicitly listed
- Classification/composition committee details, where useful

RULE:
Do not infer technical requirements from snippets or third-party sources. Preserve the official source URL and document title for every chunk.
