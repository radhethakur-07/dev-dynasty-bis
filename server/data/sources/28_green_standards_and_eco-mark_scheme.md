# 28 Green Standards and Eco-Mark Scheme

Source: https://standards.bis.gov.in/

## Eco-Mark Labelling for Environmentally Friendly Products

The Eco-Mark scheme, administered by BIS under Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) guidelines:
- Grants an earthen pot (Matka) Eco-Mark logo to products meeting strict environmental and quality standards.
- **IS 17899:2022**: Assessment of Biodegradability of Plastics in Soil and Marine Environments.
- **IS 18267:2023**: Food Serving Utensils made from Agri-By-Products (compostable tableware).
- Applies to soaps, detergents, paper, architectural paints, textiles, and packaging materials.

Official Portal: https://www.bis.gov.in/eco-mark/
