# Construction and Building Materials — Indian Standards and BIS Certification

## Overview
Building materials are heavily regulated by BIS through Quality Control Orders. Cement, steel, and structural materials are under compulsory BIS certification. Standards are developed by BIS committees in conjunction with the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs and the Indian Roads Congress (IRC).

## Cement
- **IS 269:2015**: Ordinary Portland Cement — 33, 43, and 53 grades. Compulsory under Cement QCO.
- **IS 1489 (Part 1):2015**: Portland Pozzolana Cement (Fly-ash based). Compulsory.
- **IS 1489 (Part 2):2015**: Portland Pozzolana Cement (Calcined clay based). Compulsory.
- **IS 455:2015**: Portland Slag Cement. Compulsory.
- **IS 8112:2013**: OPC 43 Grade. Compulsory. Most commonly used for general construction.
- **IS 12269:2013**: OPC 53 Grade. Compulsory. Used for high-strength concrete and pre-stressed concrete.
- **IS 3466:1988**: Masonry Cement. Compulsory.
- **IS 12330:1988**: Sulphate Resisting Portland Cement. Compulsory.
- **IS 16415:2015**: Composite Cement. Compulsory.

## Concrete
- **IS 456:2000**: Plain and Reinforced Concrete — Code of Practice. The foundational standard for structural concrete design in India.
- **IS 10262:2019**: Concrete Mix Proportioning — Guidelines.
- **IS 516:2018**: Method of Tests for Strength of Concrete (compressive, flexural, split tensile).
- **IS 383:2016**: Coarse and Fine Aggregates for Concrete. Specifies grading, deleterious materials limits, and physical property requirements.

## Bricks and Blocks
- **IS 1077:2019**: Common Burnt Clay Building Bricks. Specifies compressive strength classes (3.5 to 40 MPa), water absorption, and efflorescence.
- **IS 2185 (Part 1):2005**: Concrete Masonry Units — Hollow and Solid. Specifies load-bearing and non-load-bearing blocks.
- **IS 3952:1988**: Burnt Clay Hollow Blocks.
- **IS 12894:2002**: Fly Ash Lime Bricks.

## Plywood and Wood Products
- **IS 303:2018**: Plywood for General Purposes. Compulsory under Plywood QCO.
- **IS 710:2010**: Marine Grade Plywood. Compulsory under Plywood QCO.
- **IS 848:2006**: Specifications for Synthetic Resin Adhesives for Construction.
- **IS 12406:2003**: Medium Density Fibre Board (MDF).

## Tiles
- **IS 13630 (Part 1 to 14)**: Ceramic Tiles — Testing methods.
- **IS 15622:2017**: Ceramic Floor Tiles (Pressed, Dry Process). Compulsory under Tiles QCO.
- **IS 13712:1993**: Vitrified Tiles.

## Structural Steel
- **IS 2062:2011**: Hot Rolled Structural Steel. Compulsory.
- **IS 1786:2008**: TMT Bars. Compulsory.

## Water Proofing
- **IS 3384:2016**: Bitumen Primer.
- **IS 1580:2012**: Bituminous Compound for Waterproofing and Damp-Proofing.
