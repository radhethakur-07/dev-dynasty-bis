# Textiles — Indian Standards and BIS Certification

## Overview
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) publishes numerous standards for textiles and textile products. Some textile products fall under compulsory certification, especially those related to safety (e.g., protective clothing) or quality assurance for government procurement. The Textile Committee and BIS work together on standardization in this sector.

## Cotton Textiles
- **IS 2720:2019**: Grey Cotton Fabrics. Specifies requirements for grey cotton fabrics used in garment manufacturing.
- **IS 11702:2015**: Dyed Cotton Fabrics — Specification.
- **IS 1390**: Cotton Canvas (for tents, tarpaulins, and industrial use).
- **IS 168:2016**: Cotton Sewing Thread — Specification.

## Synthetic and Blended Textiles
- **IS 11284:2015**: Polyester-Cotton Blended Fabrics.
- **IS 11702**: Dyed Polyester and Blended Fabrics.
- **IS 12673**: Polyester Staple Fibre (Virgin).
- **IS 3468**: Nylon Fabrics for parachutes and industrial applications.

## Protective and Safety Textiles
- **IS 11871:2018**: Industrial Safety Gloves. Covers leather, rubber, and fabric gloves for industrial use.
- **IS 6994 (Part 1):2013**: Industrial Safety Footwear. Specifies requirements for toe protection, slip resistance, and material quality.
- **IS 15758:2007**: Flame Retardant Textiles — General Requirements.
- **IS 1955:2002**: Fire Resistant Textile Fabrics.

## Jute and Natural Fibres
- **IS 1332**: Jute Sacking — Specification.
- **IS 2467**: Jute Hessian Cloth — Specification.
- **IS 7205**: Coir Yarn — Specification.

## Testing Methods
- **IS 1963**: Methods of Test for Cotton Fabrics — Determination of Thread Density, Mass, and Tensile Strength.
- **IS 1969**: Methods of Test for Textiles — Determination of Colour Fastness to Washing.
- **IS 11056**: Determination of Fibre Composition (Quantitative Chemical Analysis).
- **IS 15370**: Formaldehyde Content of Textile Fabrics.

## Labelling Requirements
- **IS 11625**: Care Labelling of Textiles. Specifies laundry care symbols (wash, bleach, dry, iron, professional care) that must be displayed on textile products sold in India.

## Textile Testing Laboratories
Textile testing must be conducted at BIS-recognized or NABL-accredited laboratories. Key parameters tested include:
1. Tensile strength and elongation
2. Colour fastness (washing, rubbing, light, perspiration)
3. Dimensional stability (shrinkage)
4. Pilling resistance
5. Fabric count (threads per cm)
6. Chemical safety (formaldehyde, azo dyes, heavy metals)
