# BIS Consumer / FAQ — Initial Ingestion Notes

Source status: Official BIS public information.

## BIS Care App

https://www.bis.gov.in/bis-apps/?lang=en

Relevant capabilities listed by BIS include:
- Verify Licence Details for products bearing a BIS mark.
- Verify HUID for hallmarked jewellery.
- Know Your Standards information, licences and laboratories.
- Locations of BIS laboratories and offices.
- Access to certification-related information.

## Hallmarking Consumer Protection

https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/consumer-protection/?lang=en

Relevant official guidance includes:
- consumer testing of hallmarked/unhallmarked articles through BIS Recognized Assaying & Hallmarking Centres;
- HUID verification using BIS Care App;
- guidance concerning compensation when hallmarked jewellery is found to have lower purity than marked.
