# 29 Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme FMCS

Source: https://www.bis.gov.in/fmcs/

## FMCS Licensing Process for Overseas Manufacturers

The Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS) operates under Scheme-I of BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations:
1. Enables foreign manufacturers to use the standard ISI Mark on products exported into India.
2. Mandatory Requirement: The overseas manufacturer must appoint an Authorized Indian Representative (AIR) residing in India.
3. Process:
   - Submit online application on www.bis.gov.in/fmcs with factory layout, test equipment, and nomination of AIR.
   - BIS technical officers conduct on-site factory audit at the overseas manufacturing facility.
   - Independent sample drawing and laboratory testing in India.
   - Grant of CM/L licence upon passing test results.
4. Validity: Initial licence is granted for 1 to 2 years, renewable up to 5 years.
