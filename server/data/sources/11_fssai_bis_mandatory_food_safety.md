# Mandatory Food Safety and BIS Certification Products (FSSAI & BIS Co-Regulation)

## Legal Mandate
Under Regulation 2.3.14 of the Food Safety and Standards (Prohibition and Restrictions on Sales) Regulations, 2011, issued by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, read with the BIS Act, 2016:
No person or food business operator shall manufacture, sell, or exhibit for sale any of the specified food and beverage items except under the BIS Certification Mark (ISI Mark).

## Mandatory Certified Products

### 1. Packaged Drinking Water
- **IS 14543:2024**: Packaged Drinking Water (Other than Packaged Natural Mineral Water) — Specification.
- Mandatory for all commercial 20-litre jars, 1-litre bottles, pouches, and containers.
- Factory must have an in-house dedicated testing laboratory equipped for physical, chemical, and microbiological parameters (Total Viable Count, Coliforms, E. coli, Salmonella, Pseudomonas aeruginosa, Yeast & Mould).
- Source water must be tested for toxic heavy metals (lead, arsenic, cadmium, mercury) and pesticide residues (organochlorines, organophosphorus).

### 2. Packaged Natural Mineral Water
- **IS 13428:2024**: Packaged Natural Mineral Water — Specification.
- Must be obtained directly from natural subterranean sources (springs, artesian wells) without alteration of mineral composition.

### 3. Infant Nutrition & Milk Products
- **IS 1165:2002**: Milk powder.
- **IS 14433:2007**: Infant milk substitutes, milk-based and cereal-based foods.
- **IS 11536:2007**: Processed cereal-based complementary foods for infants.
- **IS 4812:2014**: Condensed milk, partly skimmed and skimmed condensed milk.

### Statutory Enforcement
Under the joint enforcement framework of FSSAI and BIS, running a packaged drinking water plant without a valid BIS licence (CM/L number) and FSSAI licence is an illegal operation subject to immediate factory sealing, seizure of packaged stock, and criminal prosecution.
