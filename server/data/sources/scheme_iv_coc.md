# BIS Certificate of Conformity (CoC) — Scheme IV

Source status: Official BIS Public Information.
Official source URL: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/products-under-compulsory-certification/scheme-4/?lang=en

## Scope of Scheme-IV (Certificate of Conformity)

- Regulatory Basis: Governed under Scheme-IV of Schedule-II of the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018.
- Purpose: Provides a conformity assessment mechanism for products or consignments where granting a continuous manufacturing licence (Scheme-I) is not feasible or applicable.
- Applicability: Typically applied to limited manufacturing runs, batch/consignment imports, components, or specialized items governed by statutory notifications.

## Procedure for Certificate of Conformity

1. Application: Manufacturer or importer applies with batch details, invoice, packing list, and specifications.
2. Sampling & Inspection: BIS draws representative samples from the specific consignment or manufacturing batch.
3. Laboratory Testing: Drawn samples are tested against relevant Indian Standard specifications in a BIS-approved laboratory.
4. Grant of CoC: A Certificate of Conformity is issued strictly covering the verified batch quantity upon passing laboratory tests.
