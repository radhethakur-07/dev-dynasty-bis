# Official Quality Control Orders (QCO) Compendium — Bureau of Indian Standards

## Regulatory Overview & Legal Authority
Under Section 16 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016, the Central Government, after consulting the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), notifies Quality Control Orders (QCOs) in the Gazette of India.
Once a product is notified under a QCO:
1. It becomes mandatory for every domestic manufacturer and overseas manufacturer exporting to India to conform to the specified Indian Standard (IS).
2. The product MUST bear the Standard Mark (ISI Mark under Scheme I, or CRS Registration Mark under Scheme II) under a valid licence/certificate from BIS.
3. No person, distributor, retailer, importer, or e-commerce entity shall manufacture, import, store, sell, or distribute goods that do not comply with the notified standard.

## 1. Food, Water & Consumer Safety (Ministry of Consumer Affairs & FSSAI)
- **IS 14543:2024**: Packaged Drinking Water (Other than Packaged Natural Mineral Water) — Specification. Compulsory under Food Safety and Standards (Prohibition and Restrictions on Sales) Regulations and BIS Scheme I (ISI Mark). Mandated factory in-house microbiological and chemical testing labs.
- **IS 13428:2024**: Packaged Natural Mineral Water — Specification. Compulsory under Scheme I (ISI Mark).
- **IS 2347:2017**: Domestic Pressure Cookers — Specification. Compulsory under Domestic Pressure Cooker (Quality Control) Order. Mandatory safety testing for operating pressure, burst pressure, and safety relief valve.
- **IS 4246**: Domestic Gas Stoves for use with Liquefied Petroleum Gases (LPG). Compulsory under Gas Stoves (Quality Control) Order.
- **IS 9798**: Low Pressure Regulators for use with LPG Mixtures. Compulsory under Scheme I.

## 2. Toys Safety Regulations (DPIIT Quality Control Order)
Toys (Quality Control) Order, 2020 issued by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT):
- **IS 9873 (Part 1):2019**: Safety Aspects Related to Mechanical and Physical Properties.
- **IS 9873 (Part 2):2017**: Flammability of Toys.
- **IS 9873 (Part 3):2020**: Migration of Certain Elements (heavy metals screening: lead, cadmium, mercury, arsenic).
- **IS 9873 (Part 4):2017**: Swings, slides and similar activity toys for indoor and outdoor family domestic use.
- **IS 9873 (Part 7):2017**: Requirements and test methods for finger paints.
- **IS 9873 (Part 9):2017**: Phthalates determination in toys and children products.
- **IS 15644:2006**: Safety of Electric Toys (electrical insulation, battery safety, overheating).
All toys manufactured or imported into India require Scheme I (ISI Mark) licence after factory inspection and BIS testing.
