What to ingest:
- Product
- Applicable IS number
- Certification scheme
- Whether compulsory/voluntary when explicitly stated
- QCO/notification title and date when listed
- Implementation date when listed
- Relevant official links

Do not convert "compulsory" into "BIS license required" unless the source supports the exact claim.
