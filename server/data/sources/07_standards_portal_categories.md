# BIS Standards Discovery Categories

Use the official Standards portal as the master discovery source:
https://standards.bis.gov.in/

Priority categories for the MVP knowledge base:
1. Food and agriculture
2. Drinking water and packaged water
3. Building materials / cement / construction
4. Electrical appliances
5. Electronics and IT
6. Household goods
7. Toys and children's products
8. Textiles
9. Chemicals
10. Petroleum / fuels
11. Mechanical / engineering products
12. Automotive
13. Steel / metals
14. Medical devices
15. Laboratory/testing related standards
16. Consumer products
17. Environment / sustainability
18. Energy efficiency
19. Safety-related standards

For each category, prioritize records that can support product-to-standard recommendations and certification guidance.
