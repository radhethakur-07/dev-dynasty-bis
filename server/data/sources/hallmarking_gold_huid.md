# BIS Gold Hallmarking & HUID Official Guidelines

Source status: Official BIS Public Information.
Official source URL: https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/hallmarking-faqs/hallmarking-faq/?lang=en

## Mandatory Gold Hallmarking & Scope

- Governing Standard: IS 1417 — Gold and Gold Alloys, Jewellery/Artefacts — Fineness and Marking — Specification.
- Mandatory Implementation: Under the Hallmarking Order, mandatory hallmarking on gold jewellery came into effect on 1 July 2021 in designated districts and has been progressively expanded across India.
- Exemption Threshold: Certain items like export articles, lightweight items below 2 grams, and medical/industrial items have specific statutory exemptions as per BIS notifications.

## The 3 Mandatory Hallmarks on Gold Jewellery

Since 1 July 2021, hallmark on gold jewellery consists of three mandatory marks:
1. BIS Logo (Triangle Hallmark).
2. Purity of the article in Caratage & Fineness (24K999, 23K958, 22K916, 20K833, 18K750, 14K585).
3. 6-digit Alphanumeric HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) code unique to each piece.

## HUID Traceability & Assaying Process

- HUID is a 6-digit unique alphanumeric code laser-inscribed on each item at BIS-recognized Assaying and Hallmarking Centres (AHCs).
- The HUID links the item in the central BIS digital registry to the manufacturing jeweller, the AHC that tested it, date of hallmarking, and exact purity grade.
- Violations, sale of fake hallmarks, or selling unhallmarked gold in mandatory districts attract penalties under Section 29 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016.

## Verification & Consumer Redressal

- Consumers should verify HUID authenticity using the 'Verify HUID' feature on the BIS Care App before purchasing.
- Consumers can also have their jewellery tested at BIS Recognized Assaying & Hallmarking Centres on a chargeable basis.
- If hallmarked jewellery is found to have lower purity than declared, BIS guidelines mandate that the jeweller must pay compensation to the buyer equal to two times the shortfall calculated on the purchase value.
