# Medical Devices — Indian Standards and BIS Certification

## Overview
Medical devices in India are regulated by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) under the Medical Devices Rules, 2017. BIS publishes Indian Standards for medical equipment safety, performance, and testing. The Medical Devices (Quality Control) Orders mandate BIS certification for specific categories.

## Surgical and Medical Equipment
- **IS 13450:2013**: Sterile, Single Use Hypodermic Syringes. Compulsory under Medical Devices QCO. Covers 1 mL to 50 mL capacity syringes.
- **IS 8248:2009**: Sterile, Single Use Hypodermic Needles. Compulsory. Specifies gauge, length, sharpness, and sterility requirements.
- **IS 7262:2014**: Condoms (Male Latex Rubber). Compulsory. Mandatory burst volume and pressure, freedom from holes, and tensile properties testing.

## Surgical Implants
- **IS 15714:2022**: Endotracheal Tubes — Specification.
- **IS 5765 (Part 2)**: Surgical Implants — General Requirements for marking, packaging, and labelling.
- **IS/ISO 5832 (Parts 1 to 14)**: Implants for Surgery — Metallic Materials (stainless steel, cobalt-chromium, titanium alloys).

## Diagnostic Equipment
- **IS/IEC 60601-1:2010**: Medical Electrical Equipment — General Requirements for Basic Safety and Essential Performance. Foundational safety standard for all powered medical devices.
- **IS/IEC 60601-1-2**: Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) requirements for medical equipment.
- **IS 12048:2011**: Non-Invasive Blood Pressure Monitors — Requirements.
- **IS 9919**: Pulse Oximeters for medical use.

## Personal Protective Equipment (Medical)
- **IS 9473:2002**: Surgical Rubber Gloves — Specification.
- **IS 16289:2014**: Respiratory Protective Devices — Filtering Half Masks to protect against particles (similar to N95 masks).
- **IS 17423:2020**: Face Masks (General Purpose) — Requirements and Test Methods. Published during COVID-19 pandemic.

## Dental Equipment
- **IS 6527**: Dental X-Ray Film and Processing Chemicals.
- **IS 7840**: Dental Composite Restorative Materials.

## Sterilization
- **IS/ISO 11135**: Sterilization of health-care products — Ethylene oxide.
- **IS/ISO 11137 (Parts 1 to 3)**: Sterilization of health-care products — Radiation.
- **IS/ISO 17665**: Sterilization of health-care products — Moist heat (autoclaves).

## Testing Requirements
Medical device testing must be conducted at:
- NABL-accredited laboratories
- BIS-recognized testing laboratories
- Notified laboratories under Medical Devices Rules, 2017

Key test parameters:
1. Biocompatibility (cytotoxicity, sensitization, irritation)
2. Sterility and endotoxin testing
3. Electrical safety (IS/IEC 60601-1)
4. Packaging integrity
5. Dimensional accuracy
6. Material composition and heavy metals
