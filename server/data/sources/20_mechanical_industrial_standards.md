# Mechanical and Industrial Equipment — Indian Standards

## Overview
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) publishes extensive standards for mechanical engineering, industrial equipment, and manufacturing. These standards cover steel, fasteners, pressure vessels, bearings, welding, and industrial safety equipment.

## Steel and Steel Products
- **IS 2062:2011**: Hot Rolled Medium and High Tensile Structural Steel. Compulsory under Steel QCO. Used in construction, bridges, pressure vessels, and shipbuilding.
- **IS 1786:2008**: High Strength Deformed Steel Bars and Wires for Concrete Reinforcement (TMT Bars). Compulsory under Steel QCO. Grades: Fe 415, Fe 500, Fe 500D, Fe 550, Fe 600.
- **IS 2830:2012**: Carbon Steel Cast Billet Ingots, Billets, Blooms and Slabs. Compulsory.
- **IS 277**: Galvanized Steel Sheets (Plain and Corrugated). Compulsory under Scheme I.
- **IS 10748:2004**: Hot Rolled Steel Strip for Welded Tubes and Pipes.

## Fasteners
- **IS 1363 (Parts 1 to 3)**: Hexagon Head Bolts, Screws and Nuts (ISO Metric). Property classes 4.6, 5.6, 6.8, 8.8, 10.9, 12.9.
- **IS 1364 (Parts 1 to 3)**: Hexagon Head Bolts, Screws and Nuts (Product Grades A and B).
- **IS 1367 (Parts 1 to 20)**: Technical Supply Conditions for Threaded Steel Fasteners.
- **IS 6623**: High Strength Friction Grip (HSFG) Bolts.

## Pressure Vessels and Boilers
- **IS 2825:1969**: Code for Unfired Pressure Vessels.
- **IS 803**: Code of practice for design, fabrication and erection of vertical mild steel cylindrical welded oil storage tanks.
- **IS 2585**: Portable Steel LPG Cylinders.

## Bearings
- **IS 16192:2014**: Rolling Bearings — Dynamic Load Ratings and Rating Life (ISO 281 adopted).
- **IS 3824 (Parts 1 to 4)**: Rolling Bearings — Boundary Dimensions.
- **IS 15945**: Tapered Roller Bearings — Specification.

## Welding
- **IS 814:2004**: Covered Electrodes for Manual Metal Arc Welding of Carbon and Carbon-Manganese Steel.
- **IS 1278**: Filler Rods and Wires for Gas Welding.
- **IS 3613**: Acceptance Tests for Wire Flux Combinations for Submerged Arc Welding.

## Industrial Safety Equipment
- **IS 2925:1984**: Industrial Safety Helmets.
- **IS 5983:2020**: Eye Protectors (Industrial Safety Goggles).
- **IS 6994 (Part 1 to 3)**: Industrial Safety Footwear.
- **IS 3521**: Industrial Safety Belts and Harnesses.

## Valves and Fittings
- **IS 778:2019**: Specification for copper alloy gate, globe and check valves.
- **IS 1239 (Parts 1 to 2)**: Steel Tubes, Tubulars and Other Wrought Steel Fittings.
- **IS 2062**: Steel flanges for pipes, valves, and fittings.
