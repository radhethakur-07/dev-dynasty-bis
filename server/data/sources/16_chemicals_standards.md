# Chemicals and Chemical Products — Indian Standards

## Overview
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) publishes Indian Standards for a wide range of chemical products, including industrial chemicals, paints, adhesives, pesticides, fertilizers, and specialty chemicals. Some chemical products fall under compulsory certification via Quality Control Orders (QCOs).

## Industrial Chemicals
- **IS 252:2013**: Sodium Hydroxide (Caustic Soda) — Specification. Technical and food grade.
- **IS 253:2013**: Hydrochloric Acid (Muriatic Acid) — Specification. Technical grade.
- **IS 264**: Sulphuric Acid — Specification.
- **IS 323**: Calcium Carbide — Specification.
- **IS 460**: Calcium Chloride (Technical Grade).
- **IS 513:2018**: Carbon Black — Specification.

## Paints and Surface Coatings
- **IS 15489:2013**: Ready Mixed Paint, Brushing, Acrylic Emulsion Based for Interior Use.
- **IS 15957:2012**: Ready Mixed Paint, Brushing, Acrylic Emulsion Based for Exterior Use.
- **IS 101 (Part 1 to 7)**: Methods of Test for Paints and Varnishes. Covers physical, chemical, optical, and durability testing.
- **IS 2932:2016**: Enamel, Exterior (oil-based). For exterior wood and metal surfaces.
- **IS 2933**: Enamels, Interior — Specification.

## Adhesives
- **IS 526**: Synthetic Resin Adhesive for Plywood (Phenol and Amino Formaldehyde). Compulsory under Plywood QCO.
- **IS 14823**: Cyanoacrylate Adhesives (Super Glue).
- **IS 4835**: Polyvinyl Acetate Dispersion Based Adhesive (white glue).

## Fertilizers
- **IS 306**: Ammonium Sulphate — Specification.
- **IS 10091**: Ammonium Phosphate Sulphate — Specification.
- **IS 8360 (Part 1 to 4)**: Methods of Sampling and Test for Fertilizers.

## Pesticides
- **IS 6392**: Malathion Technical — Specification.
- **IS 8190**: Cypermethrin Technical — Specification.

## Rubber and Rubber Products
- **IS 3400 (Parts 1 to 24)**: Methods of Test for Vulcanized Rubbers. Comprehensive testing covering tensile strength, hardness, abrasion, compression set, and ageing.
- **IS 5714:2019**: Rubber Hose for LPG (Domestic Use). Compulsory under Scheme I.
- **IS 3708**: Rubber Gloves for Electrical Purposes.

## Plastics
- **IS 10146:2011**: Polyethylene for Safe Use in Contact with Foodstuffs. Compulsory under QCO.
- **IS 10151**: PVC and its Copolymers for Safe Use in Contact with Foodstuffs. Compulsory under QCO.
- **IS 12252**: Polyethylene Water Storage Tanks. Compulsory under Scheme I.
- **IS 15328**: PVC Pipes for Water Supply — Specification.

## Testing Standards
Chemical testing must be conducted at NABL-accredited or BIS-recognized laboratories. Key tests include:
1. Purity and assay
2. Heavy metal content (lead, mercury, cadmium, arsenic)
3. pH value
4. Specific gravity
5. Viscosity
6. Flash point and flammability
7. Toxicity and environmental impact parameters
