## Toys (Quality Control) Order, 2020 Regulations

The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) issued the Toys (Quality Control) Order, 2020.
It is strictly illegal to manufacture, import, store, or sell any toy for children under 14 years without the ISI Mark.
- **IS 9873 (Part 1):2019**: Mechanical and Physical Safety (No sharp edges, choking hazard prevention, drop and tensile resistance).
- **IS 9873 (Part 2):2017**: Flammability testing.
- **IS 9873 (Part 3):2020**: Toxic heavy metal element migration limits (Lead, Cadmium, Mercury, Arsenic).
- **IS 15644:2006**: Electric toys safety.

Licensing: Domestic and foreign manufacturers must undergo complete factory audit and laboratory product testing under Scheme I.
