# Electronics and IT Goods — Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS / Scheme II)

## Overview
The Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS), also called BIS Scheme II, requires that specified electronics and IT products be registered with BIS before they can be manufactured, imported, sold, or distributed in India. CRS is administered by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). Unlike Scheme I (ISI Mark), CRS does not require BIS factory inspection; instead, products must be tested at a BIS-recognized laboratory and registration is granted based on test reports.

## CRS Registration Process
1. **Application**: Manufacturer/importer applies online via BIS portal (manakonline.in)
2. **Testing**: Product samples sent to a BIS-recognized lab for testing against the specified Indian Standard
3. **Test Report**: Lab submits test report to BIS
4. **Registration**: BIS issues CRS registration certificate (valid for 2 years, renewable)
5. **Marking**: Product must bear the Standard Mark for CRS (BIS Registration Mark)

## IT Equipment
- **IS 13252 (Part 1):2010/IEC 60950-1:2005**: Information Technology Equipment — Safety. Covers laptops, desktops, monitors, servers, printers, scanners, plotters, point-of-sale terminals, and UPS systems. Compulsory under CRS.
- **IS/IEC 62368-1:2018**: Audio/Video, Information and Communication Technology Equipment — Safety. New unified standard replacing IS 13252 for next-generation devices.

## Mobile Phones and Accessories
- **IS 16333 (Part 3)**: Mobile phones — Indian language support requirements. Compulsory CRS.
- **IS 16046 (Part 2):2018**: Secondary Lithium Cells and Batteries for portable devices (mobiles, laptops, tablets, power banks). Compulsory under CRS. Covers safety for lithium-ion cells.

## Consumer Electronics
- **IS 616:2017**: Audio, Video and Similar Electronic Apparatus — Safety Requirements. Compulsory under CRS. Covers televisions, set-top boxes, DVD players, audio systems, media players.
- **IS 13252 (Part 1)**: Also covers projectors and video-conferencing equipment.
- **IS 302 (Part 2, Section 15)**: Microwave Ovens — safety requirements (compulsory under CRS for certain models).

## Power Adapters and Chargers
- **IS 13252 (Part 1)**: Covers power adapters, AC-DC converters for IT equipment. Compulsory CRS.
- **IS 16046 (Part 1):2018**: Secondary Nickel Cells and Batteries — Safety Requirements.

## LED Products
- **IS 15885 (Part 2/Section 13):2012**: Fixed luminaires with LED sources. Compulsory CRS.
- **IS 16102 (Part 1):2012**: Self-ballasted LED lamps for general lighting — Safety. Compulsory under Scheme I.
- **IS 16102 (Part 2):2012**: Self-ballasted LED lamps — Performance. Compulsory under Scheme I.

## Inverters and UPS
- **IS 16242 (Part 1):2014**: Inverters and UPS up to 5 kVA (safety). Compulsory under CRS.
- **IS 16242 (Part 2):2015**: Inverters and UPS — Performance and EMC requirements.

## Solar / Photovoltaic Equipment
- **IS 16529**: Power Converters for use in Photovoltaic Power Generating Systems (Inverters). Compulsory CRS.

## CRS Fee Structure
- Application fee: ₹1,000
- Registration fee: ₹1,000
- Renewal fee: ₹1,000 (every 2 years)
- Testing fees: vary by product and lab (₹10,000–₹50,000 typical range)

## Key Differences: Scheme I vs Scheme II (CRS)
| Feature | Scheme I (ISI Mark) | Scheme II (CRS) |
|---------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Mark | ISI Mark (Standard Mark) | BIS Registration Mark |
| Factory Inspection | Yes, mandatory | No |
| Testing | BIS-recognized lab | BIS-recognized lab |
| Validity | Ongoing with surveillance | 2 years, renewable |
| Applicable To | Consumer goods, food, cement, steel | Electronics, IT goods |
