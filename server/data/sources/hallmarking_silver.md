# BIS Silver Hallmarking — Official Guidelines & IS 2112:2025

Source status: Official BIS / Press Information Bureau public information.
Official source URL: https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2050123
BIS Portal: https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/

## Standard & Implementation

- Governing Standard: IS 2112:2025 (superseding IS 2112:2014) — Silver and Silver Alloys — Fineness and Marking — Specification.
- Implementation Status: The Hallmarking Unique Identification (HUID)-based hallmarking for silver jewellery and artefacts was introduced on a voluntary basis effective from 1 September 2025.
- Alignment with Gold: The revised IS 2112:2025 standard aligns silver hallmarking with the gold hallmarking architecture by introducing digital traceability via a 6-digit alphanumeric HUID code.

## Recognized Purity Grades (Fineness in parts per 1000)

The revised Indian Standard IS 2112:2025 specifies seven permitted purity grades for silver jewellery and artefacts:
1. 999 (99.9% pure silver — newly added in IS 2112:2025)
2. 990 (99.0% pure silver)
3. 970 (97.0% pure silver)
4. 958 (95.8% pure silver — newly added in IS 2112:2025)
5. 925 (92.5% pure silver — standard Sterling Silver)
6. 835 (83.5% pure silver)
7. 800 (80.0% pure silver)

## The 3 Mandatory Marks on Hallmarked Silver

Under IS 2112:2025, every hallmarked silver article must be laser-inscribed with three distinct marks:
1. BIS Standard Mark featuring the explicit word "SILVER".
2. Purity / Fineness Grade (one of the 7 official grades, e.g., 925, 999).
3. 6-digit Alphanumeric HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) code unique to each piece.

## Consumer Verification via BIS Care App

- Consumers can verify the authenticity, declared purity grade, jewellery category, date of hallmarking, and registered jeweller / Assaying and Hallmarking Centre (AHC) details by entering the 6-digit HUID code into the official "BIS CARE" mobile application under the "Verify HUID" feature.
- Consumers can also get their silver jewellery tested for purity on a chargeable basis at any BIS Recognized Assaying & Hallmarking Centre.
