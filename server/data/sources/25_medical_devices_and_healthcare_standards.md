# 25 Medical Devices and Healthcare Standards

Source: https://standards.bis.gov.in/

## Compulsory Medical Devices & PPE Standards

In coordination with the Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO) under the Medical Device Rules (MDR), BIS prescribes mandatory standards for patient safety and medical hygiene:
- **IS/ISO 13485:2016**: Quality Management Systems for Medical Device Manufacturers.
- **IS 13422:2022**: Sterile Hypodermic Syringes for Single Use (Mandatory sterile packaging, non-toxicity, and plunger seal).
- **IS 10654:2020**: Sterile Surgical Rubber Gloves (Tensile strength, water tightness, and bio-burden limits).
- **IS 9473:2002**: Respiratory Protective Devices — N95 and FFP2 Particulate Filtering Masks (BFE ≥ 95%, breathing resistance).
- **IS 16289:2014**: Medical Textiles — Surgical 3-Ply Face Masks.

Official Portal: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/
