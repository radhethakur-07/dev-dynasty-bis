# BIS Scheme-X Certification Scheme FAQ

Source status: Official BIS Public Information.
Official source URL: https://www.bis.gov.in/scheme-x-certification/faq-scheme-x-certification/?lang=en
Scheme X Portal: https://www.scheme-x.bis.gov.in/

## Scope of Scheme-X Certification

- Regulatory Basis: Scheme-X is an advanced conformity assessment scheme notified under the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018.
- Target Sectors: Applicable to manufacturers of heavy industrial machinery, low-voltage switchgear and controlgear, transformers, rotating electrical machines, and complex industrial equipment.
- Global Scope: Applicable to both domestic Indian manufacturers and foreign manufacturers exporting industrial equipment to India.

## Certification Pathway under Scheme-X

1. Product Testing: Comprehensive type-testing of product prototypes at BIS recognized or accredited testing laboratories.
2. Technical File Evaluation: Detailed engineering review of design drawings, quality assurance manuals, bill of materials, and safety risk assessments.
3. Factory Audit: Audit of manufacturer quality control systems, production capability, and in-house inspection protocols.
4. Scheme-X License: Grant of certificate allowing the application of Scheme-X Standard Mark for the certified industrial equipment series.
