# BIS Management Systems Certification Schemes

Source status: Official BIS Public Information.
Official source URL: https://www.bis.gov.in/system-certification-overview/systems-under-certification/?lang=en

## Management System Certification Schemes Overview

The Bureau of Indian Standards operates management systems certification schemes for organizations across multiple industrial and service sectors. These certifications demonstrate compliance with international standards:
1. Quality Management System (QMS) — IS/ISO 9001: Demonstrates consistent ability to provide products and services that meet customer and applicable statutory requirements.
2. Environmental Management System (EMS) — IS/ISO 14001: Provides a framework for protecting the environment and responding to changing environmental conditions.
3. Food Safety Management System (FSMS) — IS/ISO 22000: Ensures food safety throughout the food chain up to the point of consumption.
4. Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSMS) — IS/ISO 45001: Improves workplace safety and reduces employee risks.
5. Information Security Management System (ISMS) — IS/ISO/IEC 27001: Secures organizational information assets and data confidentiality.
6. Energy Management System (EnMS) — IS/ISO 50001: Enables organizations to establish systems and processes to improve energy efficiency.
7. Medical Devices Quality Management System (MDQMS) — IS/ISO 13485: Specific requirements for regulatory compliance of medical device manufacturers.

## System Certification Assessment Process

- Application: Submission of organization profile, system manual, and applicable standard.
- Stage 1 Audit: Off-site and on-site review of management system documentation, adequacy of policies, and readiness for Stage 2.
- Stage 2 Audit: Comprehensive on-site audit evaluating implementation, operational control, and effectiveness of the management system.
- Certification Decision: Grant of license upon satisfactory resolution of non-conformities (NCs).
- Surveillance & Recertification: Periodic surveillance audits conducted every year, with complete recertification every 3 years.
